What iam.ge does

Medical documents usually end up scattered across clinic websites, email, PDFs and paper. Seeing your medical history as a whole is hard, and before a doctor's visit you gather everything by hand. iam.ge keeps every document in one place, reads it and shows it to you over time.

Storage

Lab results, prescriptions, imaging reports, discharge summaries, Form 100. Upload a PDF or just a photo. The system works out what kind of document it is and files it by patient and date.

Analysis

Values are pulled out of lab reports and placed on one chart, so you can see how they changed over the years. Units from different labs are converted for you.

Sharing

No more carrying a folder to the doctor. Send a link, and they see only what you chose to share. Revoke the link whenever you like.

Your address

Every account has its own @iam.ge address

Forward old lab results, reports and other medical documents to your personal @iam.ge address, or give it to the lab so they send results straight there. The document and its values appear on your results page within minutes. Send the same document twice and it is not added again.

name@iam.ge

Artificial intelligence

The system reads the document itself

An uploaded document is read by AI the way a person would read it, and the important parts are picked out. The result appears next to the document, so you can check it and correct anything it got wrong.

  1. 01

    Works out what the document is

    A lab report, a prescription, an imaging report or a discharge summary. If it cannot tell, it says so.

  2. 02

    Picks out what matters

    Whose document it is, when it is from, which values and units, which diagnosis and which medicines were prescribed.

  3. 03

    Links it to earlier results

    A new result is attached to the earlier values of the same marker, so you see the change straight away.

More

Family members
Your parents', children's and spouse's documents in the same account, each kept separately. Send family members' documents to the same @iam.ge address. iam.ge recognises whose each document is and adds your family members automatically.
Reminders
If a test has to be repeated regularly, you are reminded in time.
Doctors and clinics
Which doctor you saw and at which clinic, collected into one list from the documents themselves.
The original document
Next to every number, the document itself is there to check at any time.
